Medicus Vitae @VERSION@ for Mac (Apple Silicon: M1 and later)
==============================================================

A personal MCAT study app: read your books, make flashcards, review them with
FSRS, track the questions you miss, and see where you're weak.

INSTALL
-------
1. Drag "Medicus Vitae" onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. The app isn't signed with a paid Apple developer certificate, so macOS
   blocks it the first time. Allow it in one of two ways:

   a) Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities) and paste this line, then
      press Return:

        xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Medicus Vitae.app"

   or

   b) Open the app. When macOS says it can't be opened, click Done, then go
      to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click
      "Open Anyway" next to Medicus Vitae, and confirm.
      If macOS then complains about a file inside the app, use (a).

3. Open Medicus Vitae from Applications or Launchpad. Your browser opens at
   http://localhost:8080. Create your account; everything starts empty.

USING IT
--------
The app has no window of its own: it runs a small server and opens Medicus
in your browser. Close the tab whenever you like; open the app again to get
back to it. To stop the server, press Quit in Medicus's sidebar.

YOUR DATA
---------
Everything you add stays on this Mac, in
  ~/Library/Application Support/Medicus Vitae
(data, settings and logs). Replacing the app with a newer version keeps it.
Back it up with Settings > Data > Export everything.

SETTINGS
--------
To change the port or other settings, create a file named ".env" in that
folder. The settings are explained in .env.example, at
  /Applications/Medicus Vitae.app/Contents/Resources/app/.env.example
